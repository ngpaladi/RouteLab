Units and map

Trail ratings come from OpenStreetMap through Overpass, which can take half a minute for a new area; they are kept in this browser for a month. Map tiles come from the OpenStreetMap community's servers; routes from BRouter; elevation for straight lines and imports from Open-Meteo; place search from Nominatim. Each sees only the points it is asked about.

Turn cues

Off by default: most head units already say "bear left" from the line itself, and a cue at every fork buries the ones that matter.
